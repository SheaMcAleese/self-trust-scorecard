Measure what matters. Track what shifts.
This Scorecard measures ten dimensions of self-trust, grouped under the five pillars of CAANN, across your programme. You will complete it three times: at onboarding, at the midpoint, and at completion. Each round takes around five minutes. The Scorecard is not a test. It is a mirror, your own honest read on where you are now. Across the 12 weeks, the three Scorecards build a picture of measurable change in your own words. By Week 12, you will see the arc of what you have built.
